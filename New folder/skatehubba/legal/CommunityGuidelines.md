## Welcome to the SkateHubba Community!

SkateHubba is more than just an app—it's a global community where skaters of all levels come together to share their passion, learn from each other, and push the boundaries of what's possible. These Community Guidelines outline the standards we all share to keep SkateHubba a positive, inclusive, and inspiring place for everyone.

## Our Core Values

### 🛹 **Respect the Culture**
- Honor skateboarding's rich history and diverse traditions
- Celebrate creativity, innovation, and individual style
- Support skaters at all skill levels, from beginners to pros
- Recognize that skateboarding is for everyone, regardless of background

### 🤝 **Build Community**
- Help others learn and grow in their skating journey
- Share knowledge, tips, and encouragement
- Create inclusive spaces where everyone feels welcome
- Foster positive relationships within crews and the broader community

### 🌟 **Authenticity First**
- Be genuine in your interactions and content
- Share real skating experiences and honest feedback
- Give credit where credit is due
- Don't pretend to be someone you're not

### 🚀 **Innovation & Progress**
- Embrace new technology and creative expression
- Support emerging trends while respecting tradition
- Encourage experimentation and pushing boundaries
- Share discoveries that benefit the community

## Content Standards

### ✅ **What We Love to See**

**Original Skating Content**
- Your own skating videos, photos, and achievements
- Creative edits and artistic skating content
- Tutorial videos and trick breakdowns
- Spot reviews and location sharing
- Behind-the-scenes content and skating lifestyle

**Positive Community Engagement**
- Constructive feedback and encouragement
- Helpful tips and advice
- Celebrating others' achievements
- Collaborative projects and crew activities
- Educational content about skating technique, safety, and equipment

**Creative Expression**
- Digital art inspired by skating culture
- Custom avatar designs and collectible creations
- AR/VR experiences and virtual skating content
- Music, poetry, and other art forms connected to skating
- Photography showcasing skating culture and lifestyle

### ❌ **What's Not Welcome**

**Harmful Content**
- Content that promotes violence or dangerous behavior
- Harassment, bullying, or personal attacks
- Hate speech, discrimination, or exclusionary language
- Content that sexualizes minors or contains inappropriate material
- Doxxing or sharing personal information without consent

**Inauthentic Content**
- Stolen or plagiarized content without proper attribution
- Fake accounts or impersonation
- Artificially inflated engagement (fake likes, follows, etc.)
- Misleading information about products, services, or techniques
- Content that misrepresents skill level or achievements

**Disruptive Behavior**
- Spam, excessive self-promotion, or irrelevant content
- Deliberate misinformation or false safety advice
- Exploiting bugs or cheating in games and competitions
- Content that violates intellectual property rights
- Commercial activities that haven't been properly disclosed

## Safety First

### Physical Safety
- **Wear Protection:** Always promote the use of helmets, pads, and appropriate safety gear
- **Know Your Limits:** Don't encourage others to attempt tricks beyond their skill level
- **Safe Spots:** Only share legal and safe skating locations
- **Emergency Situations:** If someone is injured while skating, prioritize getting help over filming

### Digital Safety
- **Protect Personal Information:** Don't share personal details like home addresses, phone numbers, or school names
- **Report Concerns:** Use our reporting tools to flag concerning behavior or content
- **Block and Ignore:** Use platform tools to avoid problematic users
- **Think Before You Share:** Consider the long-term impact of what you post

### Mental Health and Well-being
- **Positive Environment:** Support others through failures and celebrate their successes
- **Body Positivity:** Respect all body types and skating styles
- **Healthy Competition:** Keep competitions fun and supportive rather than toxic
- **Take Breaks:** Encourage healthy platform usage and real-world skating

## Community Features Guidelines

### Crews and Social Groups

**Creating and Managing Crews**
- Crew names and descriptions must follow our content standards
- Crew leaders are responsible for moderating their communities
- Crews should be inclusive and welcoming to appropriate members
- Respect crew boundaries and don't harass crews for membership

**Crew Activities**
- Organize positive activities that build community
- Respect other crews and avoid unnecessary drama
- Use crew features for their intended purpose
- Report crews that violate community standards

### Challenges and Competitions

**Participating in Challenges**
- Follow all challenge rules and requirements
- Submit original content that you have rights to share
- Respect judges' decisions and competition outcomes
- Support fellow participants regardless of results

**Creating Challenges**
- Ensure challenges are safe and appropriate for the target skill level
- Provide clear rules and judging criteria
- Be fair and consistent in evaluation
- Promote positive community engagement

### Marketplace and Trading

**Digital Collectibles**
- Only trade items you legitimately own
- Be honest about item rarity and condition
- Complete trades as agreed upon
- Report fraudulent trading behavior

**Economic Activities**
- Follow platform rules for earning and spending Hubba Bucks
- Don't exploit bugs or loopholes in the economic system
- Respect the virtual nature of digital items
- Support the platform economy through positive participation

## Reporting and Moderation

### How to Report Issues
1. **In-App Reporting:** Use the report button on any content or profile
2. **Community Flagging:** Help us identify problematic content by flagging it
3. **Direct Contact:** Reach out to our support team for serious issues
4. **Emergency Situations:** Contact local authorities for immediate safety concerns

### What to Report
- Harassment, bullying, or threats
- Inappropriate or harmful content
- Spam or fake accounts
- Copyright infringement
- Safety violations or dangerous content
- Technical issues or bugs

### Our Response Process
1. **Review:** Our moderation team reviews all reports
2. **Investigation:** We investigate flagged content and behavior
3. **Action:** We take appropriate action based on our findings
4. **Communication:** We may communicate outcomes to reporting users
5. **Appeals:** Users can appeal moderation decisions through proper channels

### Enforcement Actions
- **Content Removal:** Removing violating posts, comments, or media
- **Warnings:** Educating users about community standards
- **Temporary Restrictions:** Limited access to certain features
- **Account Suspension:** Temporary removal from the platform
- **Permanent Ban:** Complete removal for serious or repeated violations

## Special Guidelines for Different User Types

### Young Skaters (Under 18)
- Always get parental permission before sharing personal information
- Be extra cautious when meeting online friends in person
- Focus on positive, age-appropriate content
- Report any uncomfortable interactions to adults and our platform

### Content Creators and Influencers
- Clearly disclose sponsored content and partnerships
- Follow FTC guidelines for social media advertising
- Be responsible role models for the community
- Give credit to other creators when featuring their content

### Brands and Sponsors
- Follow our guidelines for commercial content
- Engage authentically with the skating community
- Respect the culture and avoid exploitative practices
- Provide clear terms for any promotions or giveaways

### Crew Leaders and Community Moderators
- Set positive examples for your communities
- Actively moderate your spaces according to these guidelines
- Address conflicts fairly and constructively
- Communicate clearly with crew members about expectations

## Platform-Specific Guidelines

### AR/VR Experiences
- Use AR/VR features safely and be aware of your physical surroundings
- Respect others' privacy when using AR features in public spaces
- Create family-friendly AR content
- Report AR content that could be dangerous or inappropriate

### AI and Automated Features
- Don't try to manipulate AI judging systems
- Understand that AI analysis may not always be perfect
- Use AI features as intended and don't attempt to exploit them
- Provide feedback to help improve AI systems

### Live Features and Real-Time Interactions
- Be respectful in live chats and interactions
- Follow all community guidelines during live events
- Report inappropriate behavior during live sessions
- Remember that live content may be recorded or saved

## Global and Cultural Considerations

### International Community
- Respect different cultures, languages, and skating traditions
- Be patient with language barriers and communication differences
- Learn about skating cultures from around the world
- Follow local laws and customs when sharing location-based content

### Accessibility and Inclusion
- Use alt-text descriptions for images when possible
- Be mindful of users with different abilities
- Support adaptive skating and inclusive community building
- Avoid content that could trigger seizures or other health issues

### Language and Communication
- Keep discussions in commonly understood languages in public spaces
- Be patient with non-native speakers
- Use translation tools responsibly
- Avoid slang or terms that might be misunderstood across cultures

## Privacy and Personal Information

### Protecting Your Privacy
- Use privacy settings to control who can see your content
- Be selective about what personal information you share
- Regularly review and update your privacy preferences
- Understand how your data is used per our Privacy Policy

### Respecting Others' Privacy
- Don't share photos or videos of others without permission
- Respect when others don't want to be filmed or photographed
- Don't share personal information about other users
- Be mindful of background content in your posts

## Intellectual Property and Copyright

### Using Others' Content
- Always get permission before using someone else's content
- Give proper credit when sharing or remixing content
- Understand fair use limitations for copyrighted material
- Report copyright violations when you encounter them

### Protecting Your Own Content
- Understand your rights when posting content to SkateHubba
- Use copyright notices when appropriate
- Report unauthorized use of your content
- Consider watermarking valuable original content

## Seasonal and Event Guidelines

### Special Events and Competitions
- Follow specific rules for each event or competition
- Be a good sport whether you win or lose
- Support all participants and celebrate diverse achievements
- Report any unfair practices or violations during events

### Holiday and Cultural Celebrations
- Be inclusive when celebrating holidays or cultural events
- Respect different traditions and beliefs
- Avoid appropriating cultures that aren't your own
- Share positive, community-building celebration content
