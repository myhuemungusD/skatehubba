## Introduction

Design Mainline LLC ("we," "us," or "our") values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the SkateHubba mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

## 1. Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Account Data:** Username, email address, password (encrypted), date of birth, profile photo/avatar
- **Profile Information:** Display name, bio, skill level, skating preferences, location (city/region)
- **Contact Information:** Email address for notifications and account recovery
- **Device Information:** Device ID, operating system, app version, IP address

### Activity and Content Data
- **Skating Activity:** Check-ins, session logs, trick attempts, progress tracking, spot visits
- **User-Generated Content:** Photos, videos, comments, reviews, crew posts, challenge submissions
- **Social Interactions:** Friends list, crew memberships, messages, reactions, votes
- **Gameplay Data:** Achievement progress, leaderboard rankings, battle results, tournament participation
- **Wellness Data:** Injury reports, recovery tracking, health metrics (when voluntarily provided)

### Advanced Feature Data
- **AR/VR Data:** Avatar configurations, gesture recordings, AR session data, virtual spot creations
- **AI Analysis:** Video analysis results, skill assessments, style profiling, sponsorship recommendations
- **Economic Activity:** Hubba Bucks transactions, collectible trades, marketplace activity, sponsorship applications
- **Governance Participation:** Proposal votes, community fund contributions, discussion participation

### Location Data
- **Precise Location:** GPS coordinates when checking into spots (with your permission)
- **Geofenced Areas:** Entry/exit from designated skate spots for special features
- **General Location:** City/region for local community features and content recommendations

### Technical Data
- **Usage Analytics:** App interactions, feature usage, session duration, crash reports
- **Performance Data:** Device performance, AR/VR session quality, network connectivity
- **Cookies and Similar Technologies:** For authentication, preferences, and analytics

### Payment and Transaction Data
- **In-App Purchases:** Transaction history, purchase amounts, digital item ownership
- **Payment Processing:** Handled by Apple App Store, Google Play Store, or other authorized payment processors
- **Trading Activity:** Item exchange history, trade values, marketplace transactions
