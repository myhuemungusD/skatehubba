## DMCA Notice Requirements

If you believe that your copyrighted work has been infringed on the SkateHubba platform, you may submit a DMCA takedown notice. To be valid under the DMCA, your notice must include ALL of the following information:

### Required Information

1. **Your Contact Information:**
   - Full legal name
   - Mailing address
   - Phone number
   - Email address

2. **Identification of Copyrighted Work:**
   - A description of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed
   - If multiple works are involved, provide a representative list
   - Registration number if the work is registered with the U.S. Copyright Office

3. **Identification of Infringing Material:**
   - Specific identification of the material claimed to be infringing
   - Sufficient information to locate the material (e.g., username, post URL, video title, timestamps)
   - Screenshots or other evidence may be helpful

4. **Good Faith Statement:**
   - A statement that you have a good faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law

5. **Accuracy Statement:**
   - A statement that the information in your notice is accurate
   - A statement made under penalty of perjury that you are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner

6. **Electronic or Physical Signature:**
   - Your physical or electronic signature
   - If acting as an agent, include authorization documentation

### Notice Format Example

```
DMCA Takedown Notice

To: Design Mainline LLC DMCA Agent
From: [Your Name]
Date: [Date]

1. Contact Information:
   Name: [Full Legal Name]
   Address: [Complete Mailing Address]
   Phone: [Phone Number]
   Email: [Email Address]

2. Copyrighted Work:
   Description: [Detailed description of your copyrighted work]
   Registration: [Copyright registration number, if applicable]

3. Infringing Material:
   Location: [Specific URLs or identification of infringing content on SkateHubba]
   Description: [How the material infringes your copyright]

4. Good Faith Statement:
   I have a good faith belief that the disputed use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.

5. Accuracy and Authorization:
   I swear, under penalty of perjury, that the information in this notice is accurate and that I am the copyright owner or am authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.

6. Signature:
   [Your signature]
   [Printed name]
   [Date]
```

## Counter-Notification Process

If you believe your content was removed in error or misidentification, you may submit a DMCA counter-notification.

### Counter-Notification Requirements

Your counter-notification must include:

1. **Your Contact Information:**
   - Full legal name
   - Mailing address
   - Phone number
   - Email address

2. **Identification of Removed Material:**
   - Description of the material that was removed
   - Location where the material appeared before removal

3. **Good Faith Statement:**
   - A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief the material was removed due to mistake or misidentification

4. **Consent to Jurisdiction:**
   - A statement consenting to jurisdiction in the federal district court for your address (or Central District of California if outside the U.S.)
   - A statement that you will accept service of process from the person who submitted the original takedown notice

5. **Signature:**
   - Your physical or electronic signature

### Counter-Notification Process

1. **Submit Counter-Notice:** Send your counter-notification to our DMCA agent
2. **Forward to Claimant:** We will forward your counter-notification to the original claimant
3. **Waiting Period:** We will wait 10-14 business days for the claimant to respond
4. **Restoration:** If no court action is filed, we may restore the content

## Repeat Infringer Policy

### Three-Strike Policy
- **First Violation:** Warning and removal of infringing content
- **Second Violation:** Temporary account suspension and content removal
- **Third Violation:** Permanent account termination

### Factors We Consider
- Number of DMCA notices received
- Nature and severity of infringement
- User's response to previous notices
- Whether infringement appears intentional
- User's overall compliance with platform policies

### Appeals Process
Users facing account termination for repeat infringement may appeal the decision by providing evidence that:
- The takedown notices were invalid or fraudulent
- The content qualified for fair use or other legal exceptions
- Technical errors led to false positive detections
- They have taken steps to prevent future infringement

## Fair Use and Copyright Exceptions

### Fair Use Considerations
We recognize that some uses of copyrighted material may qualify as fair use under U.S. copyright law. Factors include:
- Purpose and character of use (educational, commentary, parody, etc.)
- Nature of the copyrighted work
- Amount and substantiality of the portion used
- Effect on the market for the original work

### Platform-Specific Guidelines
- **Skating Videos:** Original skating content with background music may qualify for fair use
- **Tutorial Content:** Educational use of copyrighted material may be protected
- **Commentary and Reviews:** Critical analysis typically qualifies for fair use
- **Transformative Content:** Significantly altered or remixed content may be protected

**Note:** Fair use is a complex legal doctrine. When in doubt, obtain permission from the copyright holder or consult with a qualified attorney.
